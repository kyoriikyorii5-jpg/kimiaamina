Founded in 2002 by Nobel Laureate Carl Wieman, the PhET Interactive Simulations project at the University of Colorado Boulder creates free interactive math and science simulations. PhET simulations are based on education research and engage students through an intuitive, game-like environment where they learn through exploration and discovery. Learn more.
